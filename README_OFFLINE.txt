ANRU MUSIC STUDIO PRO - 100% OFFLINE LOCAL PLAYER (v15.0 FINAL)
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS THIS VERSION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the complete, 100% offline, standalone Studio Music Player.
- All online APIs (JioSaavn, iTunes, YouTube) are completely disabled.
- All login, sign-up, password screens, and auth gatekeepers are removed.
- You can directly import songs (or an entire music folder) from your phone.
- All imported songs and their embedded covers are permanently saved in your
  phone's IndexedDB storage — you never have to re-select files on app launch!

2. KEY FEATURES INCLUDED:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 📂 Batch Music Importer: Tap "Add Songs" or "Folder" to load 50 to 500+ songs.
- 🖼️ Auto ID3 & Artwork Extractor: Extracts title, artist, and album photo.
- 🎶 Spotify-Style Queue Manager:
    * "Play Next": Plays track immediately after the current song.
    * "Add to Queue": Adds track to the bottom of the playlist.
    * Reorder: Move songs up (↑) or down (↓) to set custom playback sequence!
- 🌙 Sleep Timer: 15m, 30m, 45m, 60m, or "End of Current Song" with volume fade.
- 🎚️ 5-Band Studio Equalizer & Real Bass Boost:
    * 60Hz, 230Hz, 910Hz, 3.6kHz, 14kHz + Extra Bass Boost slider.
    * Presets: Flat, Bass Boost, Dance/DJ, Vocal, Rock.
- ⚡ Speed Controller: Listen at 0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x, or 1.5x.
- 🔍 Instant Local Search: Real-time search across your imported library.
- 📁 Custom Offline Playlists: Create custom playlists (Gym, Chill, Party).
- 🎨 Studio Themes: Aurora Dark, AMOLED Black, Cyberpunk Purple, Emerald.

3. HOW TO UPDATE ON GITHUB:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Download "Anru_Music_Studio_Pro_v15_Offline_Final.zip" from Google Drive.
2. Unzip all files.
3. Upload all files into your GitHub repository and click "Commit changes".
   (Note: Even if you do not delete old files, the new index.html only loads
   the offline modules, so nothing will conflict or interfere).
4. Open your GitHub Pages link on your phone.
5. Tap "Add Songs" or "Select Music Files", pick your downloaded songs,
   and enjoy seamless offline music streaming forever!
